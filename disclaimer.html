Specific to MetadataScrubber

It removes only what it recognises. MetadataScrubber removes the metadata it recognises in JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC files. Information stored in a form it does not recognise may stay in the file.

It cannot remove what the picture itself shows. Anything visible in the picture stays: faces, street signs, number plates, reflections and screens. It cannot change the file name you choose for the result. It cannot remove a camera's sensor fingerprint (the pattern of tiny flaws that can link photos to the camera that took them) or an invisible watermark woven into the pixels.

Check the read-back list before you share a file. After scrubbing, the app reads the new file again and lists what is left in it. That list, not this document, tells you what you are about to share.
